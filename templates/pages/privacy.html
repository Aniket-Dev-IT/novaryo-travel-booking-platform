1. Information We Collect

At Novaryo, we collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, make a booking, or contact us for support.

Personal Information:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Billing and payment information
  • Travel preferences and booking history
  • Loyalty program participation data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process your bookings and manage your account
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Send booking confirmations and travel updates
  • Improve our services and develop new features
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With travel partners to complete your bookings
  • With service providers who help operate our platform
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • With your explicit consent

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • SSL encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure payment processing
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal data

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your data
